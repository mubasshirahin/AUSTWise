# Password Manager brand icons

These files are stored locally so the Password Manager does not need third-party network requests to show platform logos.

- Most SVG marks were downloaded from the [Simple Icons project](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) via its CDN: Amazon, Discord, Facebook, Gmail, GitHub, Google, Instagram, LinkedIn, Netflix, Outlook, Pinterest, Reddit, Snapchat, Spotify, Telegram, TikTok, WhatsApp, X, and YouTube.
- Gmail and Outlook were previously low-resolution favicon PNGs; they are now the Simple Icons SVGs above.
- Daraz, Pathao, Nagad, and AUST are downloaded website logos/favicons (PNG).
- `bkash.png` is a downloaded bKash logo preview from SimilarLogo, whose record attributes it to a third-party reference.
- `rocket.svg` is from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rocket_mobile_banking_logo.svg), which records it as redrawn from Dutch-Bangla Bank's Rocket logo.

In the UI each logo is drawn on a light chip tinted with the active theme's card colour, so dark marks (GitHub, X, TikTok, Amazon) stay readable on dark themes and keep their real brand colours on light ones.

- Clash of Clans, Clash Royale, and eFootball app icons are stored from their Google Play listings (Supercell / KONAMI).
- PUBG, VALORANT, Steam, and Epic Games icons are from the [Simple Icons project](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) via its CDN.
