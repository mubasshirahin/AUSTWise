# AUSTWise — instructions

## Styling / restyle requests

When asked to restyle, polish, "better view" or "theme match" a page:

- **Do NOT add decorative shades or highlights.** No gradients (linear/radial), glows, glow box-shadows/focus rings, tinted overlays, shine effects, or accent stripes/rails.
- Keep surfaces flat: use the theme tokens as-is (`--bg-card`, `--border-primary`, `--text-*`, `--accent-*`).
- Restyle means layout, spacing, alignment, typography, sizing, wrapping/truncation fixes, and consistent use of the theme's accent color — not extra visual effects.
- **Exception — mastheads:** follow the `masthead-skill` (`.claude/skills/masthead-skill/SKILL.md`). It allows exactly two things: a 1px accent border and a faint 7% inner amber shade from the top-left. Still no outer glow.
